HOW TO READ THE RESULTS

Not one number.
A chain of assumptions.

The physical driver sets the hazard context. Exposure describes who or what sits in harm’s way. Vulnerability describes susceptibility. Preparedness and adaptation can reduce damage, but they do not remove the physical hazard.

01

Hazard

The physical process: soot-driven loss of sunlight, extreme rainfall, drought, heat, cyclone or coastal flooding.

02

Exposure & vulnerability

People, farms, roads, power systems and services that may be affected, and how susceptible they are to harm.

03

Capacity to respond

Warning systems, resilient infrastructure, food buffers, accessible healthcare, social protection and coordinated response.
