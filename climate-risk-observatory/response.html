RESILIENCE PLAYBOOK / 04

Plan for what
you can change.

Preparedness does not make the atmosphere predictable. It reduces how much a hazard can take away: life, access to services, livelihoods and the ability to recover.

01 / PRIORITIZE

Six investments with
cross-scenario value.

Start with essential services and the dependencies between them. The correct mix differs by place, budget, governance and hazard, but these action families are useful starting points.

WATCH / WARN / ACT

Early warning that reaches people

Connect forecast services to action thresholds. Use trusted local channels, accessible formats, backup communications and drills. Track lead time and who did not receive or could not act on the warning.

02 / MEASURE PROGRESS

Make resilience
auditable.

Counting plans created is not enough. Track whether essential services keep working, warnings change decisions, and protection reaches the people at greatest risk.
