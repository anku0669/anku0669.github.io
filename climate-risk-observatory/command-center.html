Model disclosure: baseline = hazard × (0.25 + 0.75 × exposure/100) × (0.25 + 0.75 × vulnerability/100). The actions case multiplies the baseline by each (1 − assumed reduction/100). The compound-pressure case uses hazard +15 and exposure +10, both capped at 100. Coefficients and intervention reductions are design assumptions, not empirically calibrated coefficients.

Interpretation boundary: these numbers are unit-less teaching indices. They do not estimate deaths, costs, event probabilities, rainfall, flood depth, crop yield or actual intervention effectiveness. Use local data and validated models for operational decisions.
