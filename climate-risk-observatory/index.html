01 / THE SIGNAL

Signals we cannot
afford to ignore.

These are separate lines of evidence, not a claim that nuclear winter and anthropogenic warming are the same process. One is an acute geopolitical shock; the other is a persistent human-driven shift in the climate system.

02 / TWO SHOCKS

Different drivers.
Connected consequences.

The useful question is not which catastrophe is scarier. It is where the mechanisms differ, where their impacts overlap, and which interventions prevent harm under multiple futures.

01 · ABRUPT SHOCK↗

Nuclear winter

Firestorms may inject black carbon into the upper atmosphere. Reduced sunlight can cool land, shorten growing seasons and disrupt rainfall, ecosystems and food production.

02 · CHRONIC FORCING↗

Hydrometeorological hazards

Warming changes the water cycle and extremes. Heavy rain, floods, drought, heatwaves, tropical cyclones and coastal surge interact with exposure and vulnerability.
