01 TWO SHOCKS / DISTINCT PHYSICS

Different drivers.
Connected consequences.

These are not the same process. Nuclear winter is a possible abrupt geopolitical shock; human-driven warming is a persistent shift in climate conditions. The practical task is to understand their distinct mechanisms and shared pressure points.

PATHWAY / NUCLEAR WINTER◉

Nuclear winter

Firestorms may loft black carbon into the upper atmosphere. Reduced sunlight can cool the surface, shorten growing seasons, alter precipitation and pressure food systems over multiple years.

PATHWAY / HYDROMETEOROLOGICAL⌁

Hydrometeorological hazards

Warming affects heat extremes and the water cycle. Heavy rainfall, floods, drought, tropical cyclones and coastal hazards interact with land use, exposure, infrastructure and preparedness.

Disruption travels
through dependencies.

Food systems require energy, water, transport and functioning markets. Health systems rely on power, logistics and safe water. Planning around single assets misses the cascade.
