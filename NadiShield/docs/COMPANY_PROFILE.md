# NadiShield — Working Company Profile

**Company name:** NadiShield (working name; subject to company-name and trademark clearance)  
**Product:** NadiShield Sentinel  
**Category:** Industrial cybersecurity workflow and evidence software  
**Stage:** Pre-company / research preview 0.1  
**Initial market hypothesis:** Small and distributed energy operators, beginning with hydropower operators and OT/SCADA service partners in India.

## Purpose

Industrial operators should be able to explain sensitive engineering activity without stitching together disconnected work orders, access logs and network observations by hand.

## Mission

Make industrial cybersecurity context and evidence easier to use for operators with small teams and constrained infrastructure.

## Vision

Become a trusted change-provenance and evidence layer for distributed critical infrastructure, beginning with one narrowly defined industrial workflow.

## Values

- Safety before feature velocity.
- Evidence before claims.
- Human accountability over automated operational action.
- Local control over sensitive site information.
- Interoperability rather than rip-and-replace.
- Honest product maturity and measurable outcomes.

## Current position

NadiShield is a product concept and development prototype, not yet an incorporated company, certified product, managed security service or production appliance. No customer, revenue, deployment, partnership or certification claim is made.

## Naming and legal checklist

Before commercial use, conduct company-name, trademark, domain and social-handle clearance in India and target markets. Obtain legal advice on incorporation, IP ownership, contracts, privacy, product liability, export restrictions and sector-specific compliance.
