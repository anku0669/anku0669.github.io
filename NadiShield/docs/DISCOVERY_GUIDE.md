# Customer discovery guide

**Purpose:** test whether the first NadiShield use case is a real, budgeted problem before expanding engineering scope.

## Interview protocol

- Interview plant operations, OT / instrumentation engineers, IT / security, SCADA integrators and maintenance vendors separately.
- Ask about the last real maintenance activity, not what they imagine they might do.
- Do not request credentials, sensitive plant diagrams or raw operational datasets.
- Ask permission before taking notes; anonymize observations.
- Separate a problem statement from a feature request.
- Do not pitch a production-ready product; explain that NadiShield is a research preview.

## Questions

1. Walk me through the last vendor or engineering maintenance intervention.
2. How was it requested, approved, scoped and closed?
3. Which identity or system proves who connected?
4. Which records show what was changed and which asset was involved?
5. How long does it take to reconstruct the event during a review?
6. What falls between your work-order system, remote access, SCADA and security monitoring?
7. What controls are already provided by your SCADA integrator or security vendor?
8. Which data must stay on site?
9. What is the operational risk of a passive monitoring deployment?
10. Who owns budget and who must approve a pilot?
11. What would make a pilot valuable enough to pay for?
12. What would cause you to reject this category of tool?

## Record per interview

- Role type and organization segment (anonymized).
- Current workflow and systems involved.
- Repeated pain points, with direct evidence where permitted.
- Existing alternative and gaps.
- Security / safety / procurement constraints.
- Budget owner, approval path and buying timeline.
- Willingness to participate in a lab or design-partner evaluation.
- Confidence level: observed fact, informed estimate or unvalidated opinion.

## Validation gates

Do not proceed to a customer-site pilot based on positive comments alone. Require repeated workflow pain, a named buyer, an integration path, explicit data boundaries and a safe pilot design. Define measurable acceptance criteria before any pilot.
