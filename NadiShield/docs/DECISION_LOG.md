# Product decision log

## 2026-10-31 — Initial wedge

**Decision:** focus the first product on maintenance change provenance and evidence correlation for industrial OT, starting discovery with small hydropower operators and SCADA integrators.

**Reasoning:** Generic asset discovery and OT anomaly detection are mature competitive areas. A narrower workflow may fit into existing security stacks and expose a concrete pain point around linking approved work to observed activity.

**Alternatives considered:** general OT network detection; AI-driven SOC; generic honeypot; broad hydropower cybersecurity platform.

**Trade-off:** narrower initial market; must prove that buyers have material workflow friction and budget.

## 2026-10-31 — Passive-first boundary

**Decision:** prototype only uses synthetic events, approved exports and passive metadata. No active scans, inline routing, automated blocking or controller commands.

**Reasoning:** industrial availability and safety take priority over detection feature velocity.

## 2026-10-31 — Public preview maturity

**Decision:** label the public website as research preview 0.1 and explicitly state missing production controls.

**Reasoning:** transparent maturity claims reduce customer confusion and avoid implied certification or operational readiness.

## 2026-10-31 — Naming and legal

**Decision:** use NadiShield as a working name only; do not claim incorporation, trademark clearance, customers, revenue or certification.

**Next validation:** legal name and trademark search, entity/IP ownership advice, customer interviews and a secure engineering review.
